# AuraFinance

AuraFinance is a private wealth tracker for your phone, tablet or computer. It keeps your cash, gold, stocks and anything else you own in one place and values them at live market prices. It shows how much each purchase has grown, records your income and transfers, moves money between accounts on a schedule, and tracks your goals and your Zakat.

**Open it:** https://qasem-ezzaldeen.github.io/personal-finance-dashboard/

This guide walks through everything you can do in the app.

## 1. Getting started

### Create your vault

1. Open the site and choose **Create a vault**.
2. Enter your email and a password (at least 6 characters), then type the password again.
3. Select **Create vault**.
4. If you're asked to confirm your email, open the link in the email we send you, then sign in.

Your vault is private: only you can see what's in it.

The first time you sign in, your vault starts with:

- four empty groups: **Cash**, **Gold**, **Stocks & ETFs** and **Other**
- an **Upcoming Income** account for money you've earned but haven't received yet
- Zakat tracking, shown on the Dashboard and in **Settings › Zakat**

### Sign in and out

- **Sign in** with your email and password.
- **Sign out** from the menu under your avatar (top right on a computer, top of the screen on a phone), or from your **Profile** page.

### Forgot your password?

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot?**
2. Enter your email and select **Send reset link**.
3. Open the link in the email **on the same device**, then choose a new password.

Reset links work once and only for a short time. If yours has expired, the page lets you ask for a new one.

## 2. Finding your way around

The app has six pages:

| Page | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your net worth, a quick income/transfer form, recent activity, how your wealth is split, and your top goals |
| **Assets** | Everything you own, grouped, with each purchase and its growth |
| **Insights** | How your net worth changed over a period and why, and your income month by month |
| **Activity** | Every income, transfer, purchase, sale and balance change, with a way to undo them |
| **Goals** | Your savings goals and milestones |
| **Settings** | How your vault works and looks: vault, appearance, accounts, automations, market & pricing, Zakat and data |

- **On a computer**, the pages are in the sidebar on the left (Settings is in your avatar menu). On wide screens the sidebar also shows their names.
- **On a phone**, the pages are in the bar at the bottom (Settings is in your avatar menu). The **➕** button above it opens a menu to **log income**, **log an hourly project**, **make a transfer** or **add an asset**.
- **The rates bar** across the top of every page shows live exchange rates, gold prices and stock prices (see [Live prices](#12-live-prices)).
- **Your avatar menu** (top right on a computer, top of the screen on a phone) shows your name and email, and links to your **Profile** (display name, email, password and sessions), **Settings** and **Sign out**.

### The Dashboard

- **Net worth:** the total value of everything you own plus Upcoming Income, in your base currency and up to two other currencies.
- **Upcoming income:** money you've logged but not received yet.
- **Change card:** by default, how much your net worth changed **this month** (since the 1st), with a short breakdown: income, markets (gold and stock prices) and currency (exchange rates). Select the **sliders** button on the card to choose what it shows and over which period (see [Insights](#insights)). Select the card itself to open Insights for the same period.
- **Zakat:** your progress to the Nisab, the day of your Hawl, or "Due now" (only if Zakat tracking is on). Select it to open all Zakat settings.
- **Quick actions** (on a computer): log income, an hourly project or a transfer between accounts without opening a popup. To record buying gold or stocks, use **Add asset**; to sell, use **Sell** on the Assets page.
- **Recent activity:** your last 8 entries. **View all** opens the Activity page.
- **Wealth distribution:** a donut chart and your asset groups. The chart fits its column (up to a fixed size) and sits beside the groups when there's room. Point at a slice to see the group's value, its share of your net worth and its [totals by unit](#14-organizing-archiving-and-hiding-assets) (e.g. grams of gold). **Details** opens the Assets page.
- **Goals:** your first three goals. **View all** opens the Goals page.

On large screens you can drag the divider between the two columns to make one wider. The app remembers your choice on that device.

### Insights

Insights shows how your net worth changed and why. Pick a period at the top: **Month** (since the 1st), **1W**, **1M**, **3M**, **YTD** (since 1 January), **1Y** or **All**.

**Measure in** shows every amount on the page in your base currency, one of your other display currencies, or **Gold** (grams of 24k gold). Measuring in another unit shows whether you're getting richer in that unit: in USD, money held in EGP loses value when the pound weakens; in gold, everything that isn't gold is worth fewer grams when gold rises (shown as **Gold's own price**). Income is always shown in your income currency.

- **Net worth change, Income, Investment gain and Exchange rates:** the totals for the period.
- **Net worth over time:** a line of your net worth for every day of the period. Green dots mark days you logged income. **By group** stacks Cash, Gold, Stocks & ETFs, Other and Upcoming Income instead.
- **What changed:** your net worth at the start, each cause of the change, and where it is today:
  - **Income:** income you logged (it counts on the day you log it).
  - **Gold prices / Stock prices:** your holdings going up or down in price. Gold is measured by its price per gram in your base currency; stocks in the currency they trade in.
  - **Exchange rates:** what cash and stocks in other currencies gained or lost in your base currency as rates moved.
  - **Other changes:** everything else, e.g. balance edits, assets you added without paying from a cash account, and money spent or moved out.
- **Prices of what you own:** the price of each karat of gold you hold (per gram), each stock, and each currency you hold money in, over the period. Dots mark your purchases (green) and sales (red), and a dashed line shows what you paid on average. The latest purchases and sales are listed under the chart.
- **How each asset did:** every gold, stock and other asset, with its value now and how much it gained or lost over the period, in money and %. On wider screens it's split into its own price and the exchange rate. The best and worst performers are marked.
- **Monthly income:** income per month for the last 12 months, in your income currency or base currency, with this month so far, last month and the average of recent full months.
- **Goal forecast:** roughly when you'd reach each goal if you kept adding your average monthly income (the last full months, up to 6) and spent none of it. Reserved goals fill one after another, as they do on the Goals page.

How it's worked out:

- What you held on each day comes from your records: purchases and sales by their dates, and cash balances by undoing later Activity entries. A purchase paid from cash takes the money out on the purchase date, even if you recorded it later.
- Past days use that day's closing prices for gold, stocks and exchange rates, with your current gold settings (premium and adjustments). The first time you open a period, past prices are looked up, which can take a moment.
- Stocks with a price override, "Other" assets and manual gold prices keep the same price throughout, so they show no price change.
- If no past price can be found for something (e.g. a stock that started trading later), Insights says so, and days before its first known price use that price.
- Before the day your vault was created, cash balances weren't recorded, so they're shown at the balance you started with. Insights tells you when a period reaches back that far.

The change card on the Dashboard can show:

| Option | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Net worth change** (default) | The change in net worth over the period, in money and % |
| **Investment gain** | Price changes of your gold and stocks over the period |
| **Income** | Income logged in the period |
| **Growth since purchase** | How much your assets have grown since you bought them (no period) |

## 3. Setting up your vault

Before adding assets, go to **Settings › Vault** and set:

- **Vault name**: shown at the top of the app.
- **Base currency**: net worth, totals and Zakat are shown in this currency.
- **Income currency**: the currency income is logged in. You can only change it while Upcoming Income is empty.
- **Also show totals in**: up to 6 more currencies. The first one appears next to your totals, and their exchange rates appear in the rates bar.
- **Number format**: how numbers look, e.g. `1,234.56`, `1.234,56` or Arabic digits. Amounts of 100,000 or more are shown without decimals.
- **Time zone**: decides when your day starts. It's used for dates, the day automatic transfers run, and the daily Zakat check. **Use this device's** sets it in one tap.

Select **Save vault settings**.

## 4. Adding your assets

Select **Add asset** on the Assets page, or **➕ › Add asset** on a phone. You can also select **Add to [group]** at the bottom of any group. **Add asset** is also how you record **buying more** of something you already have (see [Buying, selling and growth](#5-buying-selling-and-growth)). Then choose a type:

### Cash (bank accounts, wallets, savings)

1. Enter a **name** (e.g. "QNB Savings").
2. Choose the **currency**.
3. Enter the **balance now**.

### Gold

1. Choose **24k · Ingots** or **21k · Scrap gold**. The name fills in as **Ingots** or **Scrap Gold**; type your own to change it.
2. Optionally, under **What you own now**, enter the **grams**, the **date you bought it**, what you paid, and **Paid from** (see the next section).

### Stock / ETF

1. Type a **ticker** (e.g. `SPUS`, `AAPL`) or a company name (e.g. `Apple`), or tap one of the suggestions.
2. Wait for the green check showing the stock's name and current price. You can only save once the ticker has been found.
3. Optionally enter the **shares**, the **date bought** and the **total price paid**.

### Other (crypto, a car, anything)

1. Enter a name.
2. Enter the **value of one unit today** and its currency. Update this whenever the value changes.
3. Optionally enter the quantity, the date bought and the price paid.

For gold and stocks you can leave the price paid empty. The app then looks up the market price on the date you bought it and uses that to track growth (see the next section).

For every type you can also pick a **color** (used in the chart), add a **note**, and turn on **Hide when empty**.

### Editing or deleting an asset

- On the Assets page, select the **pencil** next to an asset. On the Dashboard, select the asset itself.
- You can change its name, color and note.
- **Cash accounts:** you can change the **current balance**. The change is recorded in Activity as a balance update. The currency can only be changed while the balance is zero.
- **Delete** removes the asset and all its purchases and sales. Whatever its purchases took from your cash accounts goes back to them. Past history entries stay, but can no longer restore this asset.

## 5. Buying, selling and growth

Gold, stocks and other assets are made up of **purchases** (each time you bought some) and **sales**. What you hold is everything bought minus everything sold. This lets the app show how every purchase has grown.

On the **Assets** page, select a gold, stock or other asset to expand it. Its history shows purchases and sales together, newest first. Each purchase shows:

- the **date bought** and the **amount** (grams, shares or units)
- **paid X → now Y**, and a green or red growth percentage
- **≈ … est.** when the price paid is an estimate taken from that day's market price
- **looking up the price on that date…** while an estimate is being fetched
- **add price paid** when there's no price yet

Below the purchases you'll find the current value **per gram**, **per share** or **per unit**.

### Buy (with Add asset)

Everything you buy is recorded with **Add asset**, whether it's new or more of something you have.

1. Select **Add asset** and choose the type.
2. **Buying more of something you have?** It's added to that asset instead of creating a second one:
   - **Stocks:** type the ticker. If you already hold it (e.g. SPUS), the form says *This adds to SPUS ETF*.
   - **Gold:** gold of the karat you choose is added to your existing gold of that karat.
   - **Any type:** pick the asset under **Add to**, or choose **A new asset** to keep it separate.
3. Enter the **grams**, **shares** or **quantity** and the **date bought**.
4. Choose **Paid from**:
   - **A cash account:** enter the **total paid** in that account's currency (**Use market price** fills in today's price). The money leaves that account, and you can't spend more than it holds.
   - **Not from my cash accounts:** for gold or shares you already had, a gift, or money from outside your accounts. Your cash doesn't change. Leave the price empty to use the market price on that date.
5. Select **Add asset** (or **Add to [asset]**).

For example, to record buying 3 more SPUS shares with money from nsave: **Add asset** › **Stock / ETF** › `SPUS` › **Paid from: nsave** › 3 shares › **Add to SPUS ETF**. The money is taken from nsave and the shares are added to SPUS.

The preview shows the holding and the cash account before → after. Your wealth distribution and totals update right away.

### Sell

Select **Sell** next to the asset on the Assets page.

1. Enter how much you sold, or select **Sell all**.
2. Choose the cash account the **money goes to** and the **total received** (**Use market price** fills in today's price).
3. Pick the **date sold** and select **Sell**.

The preview shows the holding and the cash account before → after, and your **gain vs. what you paid**. Each sale appears in the asset's history with what you received and its gain. Select a sale to **remove** it if it was recorded by mistake: the money is taken back out of the cash account and the holding comes back.

### Edit or delete a purchase

- **Edit:** select a purchase. You can change the amount, the date, the price paid (and its currency) and the note. If the price is estimated, type the real price to replace it. Editing never changes your cash accounts, even for a purchase paid from one.
- **Delete:** open it and select **Delete**. The asset's total goes down by that amount. If it was paid from a cash account, what you paid goes back to that account. A purchase can't be deleted if part of it has been sold; remove the sale first.

### Growth

Growth is measured in the currency you paid in, so currency changes don't hide how the asset itself performed. After a sale, growth covers what you still hold, at your **average cost** (every unit you bought counts as costing the same). Each group and your whole vault also show combined growth. You can see it in the Assets side panel, and on the Dashboard's change card when it's set to **Growth since purchase**.

## 6. Logging income

Income goes into **Upcoming Income**: money you've earned but haven't received yet. It's included in your net worth but **not** in Zakat. When the money arrives, move it to a cash account (see the next section).

### A fixed amount

1. Open **Quick actions** on the Dashboard, or **➕ › Log income** on a phone, and choose **Income**.
2. Enter the amount (in your income currency) and an optional description (e.g. "Client project").
3. Select **Add income**.

### An hourly project

1. Choose **Hourly**.
2. Enter the **hours** and **minutes** worked and your **hourly rate**.
3. The amount is calculated for you. Add an optional description and select **Add income**.

The preview shows the amount in your base currency and Upcoming Income before → after.

## 7. Moving money between accounts

You can move money between your cash accounts and Upcoming Income. (To spend cash on gold, stocks or other assets, use **Add asset** and choose **Paid from**.)

1. Choose **Transfer** in Quick actions or **➕ › Transfer**. You can also select the **Upcoming Income** bar on the Assets page or Dashboard.
2. Pick the **From** and **To** accounts.
3. Enter the amount, or select **Transfer all** to move everything.
4. Add an optional note and select **Transfer**.

If the two accounts use different currencies, the amount is converted at the current rate. The preview shows how much arrives and both balances before → after. You can't move more than an account holds.

## 8. Automatic monthly transfers

Automatic transfers move money between accounts on the same day every month, even while the app is closed. A typical use is moving all your Upcoming Income to your bank on payday.

Go to **Settings › Automations** and select **New rule**. You need at least two accounts: Upcoming Income plus one cash account.

1. **Name** the rule (e.g. "Payday sweep").
2. Choose **when**: any day from the 1st to the 28th, or the **last day** of every month.
3. Choose the **From** and **To** accounts.
4. Choose **Everything in it** or **A fixed amount**.
5. Select **Save rule**.

- New or rescheduled rules start from their next date.
- If the app wasn't able to run a rule on time, it catches up the missed month.
- Use the switch on a rule to **pause** or **turn it back on**.
- Each rule shows when it **last ran**. If a run couldn't complete (e.g. an account was deleted), a warning explains why.
- **Edit** a rule to change it, or **Delete** it. Transfers it already made stay in your Activity.

Automatic transfers appear in Activity with an **Automatic** badge.

## 9. Activity history and undoing changes

The **Activity** page lists every change, grouped by month:

- income you logged
- transfers, with the converted amount when currencies differ
- automatic transfers
- purchases paid from cash, and sales
- refunds from deleted purchases, and removed sales
- balance changes you made to cash accounts
- imported history from the previous AuraFinance

Entries show how Upcoming Income changed (before → after) where that applies.

**Filter** the list by **type** (Income, Transfers, Automations, Purchases, Sales, Balance changes, Imported history) or by **account or asset**. Select **Load more** to see older entries.

### Reverting to an earlier point

Made a mistake? Select any entry, on the Activity page or in the Dashboard's recent activity, to **revert to that point**.

1. The app shows how many entries after it will be undone, how each balance will change back, and which holdings change (e.g. a purchase undone takes its grams away, a sale undone brings its shares back).
2. Select **Revert** to undo and remove every entry after the one you picked.

This can't be undone. Imported history entries can't be reverted.

## 10. Goals

Go to **Goals** and select **New goal**.

1. Enter a **name** (e.g. "Emergency fund") and pick an **emoji**.
2. Choose what it's **measured in**: any currency, or **grams of 24k gold**.
3. Enter the **target**.
4. Choose whether to **count Upcoming Income** toward it.
5. Choose whether to **reserve money for this goal** (see below).
6. Select **Save goal**.

Each goal shows a progress bar, a percentage, how much you have **now**, and how much **remains**. It shows **Goal reached** once you get there.

**Reserved goals and milestones**

- **Milestones** (not reserved) measure your whole wealth, converted into the goal's currency or into grams of gold. Use them for targets like "reach 2M in net worth". They're never affected by other goals.
- **Reserved goals** (marked **Reserved**) don't share money. They're filled in the order of your list: the first takes what it needs, the next gets what's left, and so on. With 1M and two reserved goals of 1M each, the first is reached and the second is at 0%. Drag a goal higher to fund it first.
- A reserved goal that doesn't count Upcoming Income only uses money that isn't Upcoming Income.

- **Reorder** goals by dragging the handle.
- **Edit** or **delete** a goal with its pencil button.

## 11. Zakat

Zakat tracking is **on by default**. Everything about it is in **Settings › Zakat** (select the Zakat card on the Dashboard to get there): turning it on or off, your Nisab and Hawl, payments, and the gold price it uses.

How the app calculates Zakat:

- **Nisab:** 85 g of 24k gold, priced with your gold settings.
- **Hawl:** 354 days (one lunar year) with your wealth at or above the Nisab.
- **Zakat due:** 2.5%.
- **What counts:** everything you own except Upcoming Income.

### What happens automatically

Once a day the app checks your wealth:

- If it has reached the Nisab and no Hawl is running, a **Hawl starts**, and your wealth that day is recorded.
- If it drops below the Nisab, the Hawl **resets**.
- When the Hawl reaches 354 days, **Zakat is due**. A banner appears on the Dashboard with the amount in your base currency (and your first extra currency).

### Nisab & Hawl

The **Nisab & Hawl** card in Settings › Zakat shows your wealth in grams of gold and a progress bar, with one of these statuses:

- how far you are **below the Nisab**
- **above the Nisab** (the Hawl starts at the next daily check)
- **Day N of 354**, and how many days until it's due
- **Zakat is due**

### Editing your Hawl

If you already know when your Hawl started, select **Edit Hawl**:

1. Enter the **days** your wealth has been at or above the Nisab, or pick the **start date**. Shortcuts: *Just started*, *90 days*, *Half a year*, *Full Hawl*.
2. Tell the app what your wealth was when the Hawl started:
   - **This is my first year above the Nisab:** Zakat is based on the value of 85 g of 24k gold on the start date. The app looks up that day's gold price for you.
   - **Otherwise:** enter your wealth on the start date (or select **Use today's wealth**). Zakat will be 2.5% of it. Leave it empty to use your wealth on the day Zakat is due.
3. Select **Save**. **Clear** removes the Hawl.

### Paying Zakat

When Zakat is due, select **Mark as paid**. Confirm the amount, add an optional note, and select **Record payment**. A new Hawl starts from today if you're still above the Nisab.

Your payments are listed under **Zakat payments** in Settings › Zakat.

## 12. Live prices

The **rates bar** at the top of every page shows:

- **exchange rates** for every currency you use and every extra display currency (e.g. `USD/EGP`)
- **gold 24k and 21k per gram** in your base currency, including your local premium (only the karats you hold)
- **stock prices** for every ticker you hold or follow

Each price has an arrow showing whether it went up or down. Hover over a price to see when it was last updated. Prices that may be out of date are faded. If the prices don't fit, the bar scrolls slowly on its own; hover or touch it to pause.

- **Refresh prices:** select the **⟳** button. On a computer, it also shows how long ago prices were updated.
- **Follow a stock** you don't own: select **+ Follow**, type a ticker, and select **Follow**. To stop following it, select the **✕** on its price or go to **Settings › Market & pricing**.
- **Change gold pricing:** select a gold price to open the gold settings.

Prices also update on their own every few minutes, both on the server and while the app is open.

## 13. Gold and stock pricing settings

Go to **Settings › Market & pricing**.

### Gold

- **Live price:** uses the global gold price plus a **local premium** (0% by default). Change the premium to match your local market.
- **Set it myself:** enter your own 24k (or 21k) price per gram and its currency. 21k is always 87.5% of 24k. A **Manual** badge shows in the rates bar.
- **Per-gram adjustments:** add or subtract a fixed amount per gram to show what your gold is really worth to you. For example, −30 for what you'd get selling 21k jewelry, or +30 for 24k ingot cashback.

A preview shows the market price and what **your** gram is worth for both karats before you select **Save gold pricing**.

### Stocks

- **Followed stocks:** see, add or remove tickers you follow.
- **Manual stock prices:** set your own price per share (in USD) for a ticker you hold or follow. This replaces the live price. Select **Use live price** to go back.

## 14. Organizing, archiving and hiding assets

- **Reorder** groups and assets by dragging the handle next to them. The order is saved and shows everywhere.
- **Expand or collapse** a group by selecting its header. The app remembers which groups are open.
- **Totals by unit:** each group's header also adds up what it holds in its own unit, next to the value in your base currency. They also appear in the Assets side panel and when you point at the chart.
  - **Gold:** grams held, with 24k and 21k kept separate (e.g. `24k 14 g` `21k 40 g`).
  - **Cash:** balances per currency (e.g. `$4,700.50` `EGP 45,000.00`).
  - **Stocks & ETFs:** the value of your holdings in the currency they're priced in (e.g. 4 SPUS worth $240 and 2 SPTE worth $102 show `$342.00`). Stocks priced in different currencies get separate totals. Stocks without a price yet aren't counted.
  - **Other** isn't added up, since its assets don't share a unit.
  - A group whose only total is in your base currency, or in the second currency shown under its value, doesn't repeat it there (phones, which don't show that second line, still show it).
- **Hide when empty:** turn this on for an asset (in its edit popup) to keep it out of the list while its balance is zero. A **Show N empty** link at the bottom of the group shows them again.
- **Rename or recolor a group:** go to **Settings › Accounts**, select a group, change its name or chart color, and save.
- **Archive an asset:** in **Settings › Accounts**, select **Archive**. It's no longer counted or shown, but it isn't deleted. **Restore** brings it back. A cash account has to be empty before you can archive it.

## 15. Your profile, appearance and security

### Profile

Open it from your avatar menu.

- **Your details:** your **display name** (shown in the menu) and **avatar color**. Select **Save details**.
- **Email address:** enter a new email and select **Change email**. The change takes effect once you confirm it from the link sent to the new address.
- **Change password:** enter the new password twice.
- **Sessions:** **Sign out** of this device, or **Sign out of all devices** (every phone, tablet and computer).

### Settings › Appearance

- **Theme:** **Light**, **Dark**, or **Device** (follows your phone's or computer's setting).
- **Color palette:** **Pastel** (the original), **Minimalist**, **Sea & Beach**, **Autumn**, **Nature & Greenery** or **OLED**. Each card previews the palette; it colors the whole app, including backgrounds, your assets, the chart and progress bars. Gains stay green and losses red in every palette.
  - **OLED** is pure black with vivid colors, made for OLED screens. It's always dark: the Light/Dark choice comes back when you pick another palette.
- **Hero color:** the color of buttons, links, the selected page and highlights. Pick one of the suggested colors or **Custom**, for each palette separately. **Use [palette]'s own** goes back to the palette's color. Text stays readable whatever you pick.
- **Animations:** **Device**, **Off**, **Slow**, **Normal** or **Fast**. Select **Replay** to preview the speed.

Changes apply immediately and follow you to every device you sign in on.

## 16. Backing up, restoring and importing your data

Go to **Settings › Data**.

### Export

- **Full backup (JSON):** everything in your vault, including your full history, in one file. It can be restored later, in this vault or another one.
- **Spreadsheets (CSV):** two files you can open in Excel or Google Sheets, one for your activity and one for your assets (sales show as negative quantities). These are for reading only and can't be restored.

### Restore from a backup

1. Under **Restore from a backup**, select **Choose backup file** and pick a full backup (JSON) downloaded from AuraFinance, from this vault or another one.
2. The app checks the file and shows what's in it: the vault name, when it was saved, and how many assets, purchases, sales, goals, automatic transfers and history entries it has.
3. Select **Restore this backup** and confirm.

- Restoring **replaces everything** in this vault: assets, balances, history, goals, Zakat and settings. Your display name, email and password stay.
- A backup of your current vault **downloads first**, just in case.
- Automatic transfers restart from their next date, so restoring never moves money by itself.
- History from backups made before this update comes in read-only (it can't be reverted to). Newer backups restore history you can revert.

### Import from the previous AuraFinance

If you used the earlier version of AuraFinance with the same account, you can bring your data over once.

1. The app shows a table comparing each asset's value in the old app and the new app at today's prices. It also lists the goals, history, automatic transfers and Zakat Hawl it will bring over.
2. Check the numbers, then select **Import my data** and confirm.

- Import only works into an **empty** vault, and only once. Your old data isn't changed.
- Gold and stocks arrive as an **"Opening balance"** purchase. Afterwards, open **Assets** and set the real purchase date on each one (and the price paid, if you know it) so their growth is tracked from that day.

## 18. Tips and FAQ

**Why isn't one of my assets counted in the total?**
Its price hasn't arrived yet (e.g. a new stock). The Assets page names it in a note under the total, and it's added as soon as a price is available.

**Why is "Growth since purchase" empty?**
Growth needs a purchase price. For gold and stocks, set the date bought and the price is filled in from the market. For "Other" assets, enter the price paid.

**Why isn't my Upcoming Income counted for Zakat?**
It's money you haven't received yet. Once you transfer it into a cash account, it counts.

**I use the app on two devices. Do they stay in sync?**
Yes. Any change you make shows up on your other open devices within moments.

**Can I undo a transfer or income I logged by mistake?**
Yes. In **Activity**, select the entry **just before** the mistake and revert to it (see [section 9](#9-activity-history-and-undoing-changes)).

**I recorded a purchase or sale by mistake. What do I do?**
Delete the purchase (the money goes back to the account it came from) or remove the sale (its money is taken back out). Or revert to the entry before it in **Activity**.

**I bought gold before I started using the app. Will adding it take money from my accounts?**
No. Choose **Not from my cash accounts** when you buy, or enter it when you add the asset.

**Can I change my base currency later?**
Yes, at any time in **Settings › Vault**. All totals are converted automatically.
